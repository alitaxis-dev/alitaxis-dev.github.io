General & Advanced Payment Terms & Conditions

Due to a high number of cancellations, the following terms and conditions apply to bookings made using an Ali Taxis payment link.

1. Booking Confirmation

  • Your booking is not confirmed until payment has been received in full.
  • If another customer books the requested time slot before payment is received, your booking request may be cancelled.
  • In this situation, the payment link may expire and you will need to contact Ali Taxis again to check availability.
  • Payment alone does not confirm a booking unless the requested journey and availability have been agreed.

2. Non-Refundable Payments

Payments are non-refundable where a customer cancels a confirmed booking or fails to attend the agreed pickup.

If you cancel your confirmed booking or fail to attend your scheduled pickup, your payment will be forfeited, subject to applicable statutory consumer rights.

Your requested time slot has been reserved for you and other booking requests may have been declined as a result.

3. Amendments to Bookings

If you wish to make changes to your booking, including:

  • Date
  • Time
  • Pickup location
  • Destination
  • Number of passengers

All changes are subject to availability and may incur additional charges. Please contact Ali Taxis as soon as possible to request an amendment.

A requested change is not confirmed until it has been agreed by Ali Taxis.

4. Waiting Time Policy

A maximum of 6 minutes of waiting time is included, starting from the agreed pickup time.

During this period, the driver will make a minimum of three attempts to contact you using the contact details provided with your booking.

Customers should ensure their phone is switched on and available at the time of pickup.

5. No-Show Policy

If all passengers are not inside the vehicle within the 6-minute waiting period, the booking will be treated as a no-show.

  • The journey may proceed with the passengers who are present, where appropriate and agreed.
  • If the journey cannot proceed because the booked passengers have not arrived, the booking may be cancelled.
  • Your payment will be forfeited where the booking is treated as a customer no-show, subject to applicable statutory rights.
  • No refund will be issued where the non-refundable cancellation or no-show terms lawfully apply.

6. Passenger Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

  • Being ready at the agreed pickup location and time.
  • Providing a correct and working contact telephone number.
  • Ensuring all passengers are ready to travel.
  • Informing Ali Taxis promptly of any problems or delays.
  • Checking all booking details before making payment.

7. Acceptance of Terms

By making payment through an Ali Taxis payment link, you confirm that you have been given the opportunity to read and understand these pre-payment terms and conditions.

Payment confirms your acceptance of these terms, subject to any statutory consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

IMPORTANT – PLEASE READ BEFORE MAKING PAYMENT

Please ensure you are ready to depart at your booked pickup time. The six-minute waiting period starts from the agreed pickup time, not from the time you arrive at the pickup location.

If you need to amend or cancel your booking, contact Ali Taxis as soon as possible.
