Legal Information & Terms

Please read the following information before booking a journey with Ali Taxis. These terms explain our booking arrangements, cancellation policy and passenger responsibilities.

1. About Ali Taxis

Ali Taxis provides pre-booked private hire journeys, local taxi journeys and airport transfers around Paddock Wood, Pembury, Five Oak Green, Horsmonden, Goudhurst and surrounding areas.

All journeys are subject to availability and applicable private hire licensing requirements.

2. Bookings and Contact

Bookings should be made in advance wherever possible. WhatsApp is our preferred method of contact and booking.

A booking is only confirmed once the journey details and arrangements have been agreed with Ali Taxis.

Customers should provide accurate information, including:

  • Pickup address and destination.
  • Pickup date and agreed time.
  • Number of passengers.
  • Number and size of suitcases and other luggage.
  • Flight details and terminal information for airport collections.
  • Any special requirements that may affect the journey.

Customers are responsible for checking that all booking details are correct.

3. Fares and Payments

Fares are agreed at the time of booking wherever possible. The quoted fare applies to the journey and vehicle arrangements agreed with the customer.

  • Additional stops or changes to the journey may result in extra charges.
  • Airport parking, drop-off and other applicable airport charges may be additional unless agreed otherwise.
  • Night-time or out-of-hours premiums may apply where advised at booking.
  • Larger vehicle requirements may be subject to a different fare.

Any additional charges should be discussed with the customer before they are incurred wherever reasonably possible.

4. Airport Transfers

Customers booking airport transfers must provide accurate flight information, including the airline, flight number and arrival terminal where applicable.

Customers should keep their mobile phone available on the day of travel so that pickup arrangements can be coordinated.

Airport pickup arrangements may be affected by flight delays, terminal changes, traffic, airport restrictions and parking arrangements.

5. Cancellations, No-Shows and Refunds

Customer cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable.

  • If a customer cancels a confirmed booking for any reason, no refund will be provided.
  • If a customer fails to arrive at the agreed pickup location at the agreed time, without contacting Ali Taxis, the booking will be treated as a no-show.
  • No refund will be provided for no-show bookings.
  • Customers should notify Ali Taxis as soon as possible if they need to cancel or amend a booking.
  • All cancellations must be communicated directly through WhatsApp, telephone or email.
  • Where Ali Taxis cancels a confirmed booking or is unable to provide the agreed service, an appropriate refund will be provided where legally required.

Nothing in this policy affects statutory consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

6. Airport Waiting Time

For airport collections, up to 60 minutes of waiting time is included, starting from the actual aircraft landing time rather than the scheduled arrival time.

Additional waiting beyond the included period may incur extra charges. Any applicable additional charges will be communicated to the customer.

Customers should contact Ali Taxis if they experience problems with immigration, baggage collection or other delays after landing.

7. Passenger Responsibilities

Passengers are expected to:

  • Be ready at the agreed pickup location and time.
  • Wear seat belts throughout the journey.
  • Follow reasonable safety instructions from the driver.
  • Behave respectfully towards the driver and vehicle.
  • Ensure children are appropriately supervised.
  • Inform Ali Taxis in advance about any special travel requirements.

8. Vehicle Capacity and Luggage

The standard vehicle can accommodate up to four passengers, subject to available luggage space.

A larger or six-seater vehicle may be available by prior arrangement and must be confirmed before the booking is accepted.

Customers must provide accurate passenger and luggage information when booking. Ali Taxis cannot guarantee that all luggage will fit if the information supplied is inaccurate.

9. Passenger Conduct and Vehicle Cleanliness

Passengers must treat the driver and vehicle with respect. Smoking, including the use of electronic cigarettes and vaping, is not permitted inside the vehicle.

Passengers may be held responsible for damage, excessive mess or specialist cleaning costs caused by their actions, subject to applicable law.

10. Pets and Assistance Animals

Customers travelling with pets should contact Ali Taxis before booking to discuss arrangements.

Assistance dogs will be accommodated in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

11. Delays and Circumstances Beyond Our Control

Ali Taxis will make reasonable efforts to provide journeys at the agreed time. However, delays may occur due to circumstances outside our control, including:

  • Heavy traffic or road closures.
  • Accidents or road incidents.
  • Severe weather conditions.
  • Airport or public transport disruption.
  • Vehicle breakdowns or unexpected operational issues.

Where possible, customers will be informed of significant delays and alternative arrangements will be considered.

12. Lost Property

Customers should check the vehicle carefully before leaving. If an item is left behind, contact Ali Taxis as soon as possible with a description of the item and journey details.

Reasonable efforts will be made to locate and return lost property.

13. Accessibility and Special Requirements

Customers with accessibility requirements or special travel needs should discuss these with Ali Taxis before confirming a booking.

This helps ensure that suitable arrangements can be considered before the journey takes place.

14. Safety and Right to Refuse a Journey

Ali Taxis reserves the right to refuse or discontinue a journey where it is reasonably necessary for safety, legal or licensing reasons, including threatening, abusive or dangerous behaviour.

Any such decision will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

15. Liability

Ali Taxis will take reasonable care when providing its services. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability where it would be unlawful to do so.

Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights as a consumer.

16. Privacy and Personal Information

Personal information supplied when making a booking will be used for booking administration, communication and providing the requested transport service.

Customer information will be handled in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation.

Customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information when making a booking.

17. Complaints and Customer Enquiries

If you have a question, concern or complaint about a journey, please contact Ali Taxis directly.

WhatsApp is the preferred contact method. Please include your journey date and relevant booking details so that the matter can be looked into.

18. Changes to These Terms

Ali Taxis may update these terms from time to time. The latest version published on this website will apply to future bookings.

19. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes will be dealt with by the courts of England and Wales, subject to applicable consumer protection rights.
